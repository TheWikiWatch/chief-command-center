# Home

Start here. Your chief keeps this Second Brain with you; mostly you ask, and it answers from here.

| Where | What |
| --- | --- |
| `drop/` | Put any file here (a PDF, a photo, notes). Your chief files it away within half an hour. |
| `boards/` | Your task boards: Backlog, This Week, In Progress, Waiting On, Next Week, Done |
| `wiki/` | What your chief knows: people, projects, ideas, decisions, daily notes, reviews |
| `raw/` | The sources it learned from, kept exactly as they came |
| [[Current Analysis]] | Where things stand, and what your chief needs from you |

Ask things like "what's due this week?", "remind me to…", "what do we know about…?", or answer the questions in the morning brief.
